Failure test:

- It fails if archival images are used as wallpaper rather than evidence.
- It fails if AI reconstructions invent details or are not clearly disclosed.
- It fails if scripts paraphrase one article instead of triangulating sources.
- It fails if a solo creator tries to publish three 25–40 minute films per week.
- Defensible starting cadence: one 10–14 minute researched film per week for the
  first 12 weeks; raise output only after the workflow is measured.

## Decision from the stress test

Everyday-life history is the only finalist that combines multiple recent entrant
proofs, evergreen topic depth, a large English-language addressable audience,
readily available rights-cleared archival assets, and comparatively low policy
risk. Its main weakness is production time; the correct response is a lower,
higher-quality cadence, not mass production.
